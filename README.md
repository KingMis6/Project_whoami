# 🎭 How to Play "Who Am I?" (Game Guide)

Welcome to **Who Am I?** — a multiplayer party game for 2 to 6 players!

In this game, every player secretly submits a character (superhero, celebrity, cartoon, anime, movie character, etc.). The app shuffles these submissions and assigns one to each player. **Everyone can see who you are EXCEPT YOU!** 

Your goal is to deduce your secret character identity by asking yes/no questions out loud and submitting your guess in the app.

---

## 📱 Quick Game Setup

1. **Open the App** & enter your **Player Name**.
2. **Create or Join a Room**:
   - **Host**: Tap **Create Room**. You will receive a unique 5-letter **Room Code** (e.g., `B7X9K`). Share this code with your friends!
   - **Players**: Type the **Room Code** and tap **Join Room**.
3. **Lobby**: Once all players (2–6 people) are in the lobby, the host taps **Start Game**.

---

## 🔁 Step-by-Step Gameplay Flow

```
┌─────────────────┐      ┌─────────────────┐      ┌─────────────────┐      ┌─────────────────┐
│  1. Create/Join │ ---> │ 2. Submit Name  │ ---> │ 3. Ask Questions│ ---> │ 4. Guess & Win  │
│  Lobby (2-6)    │      │  & Auto-Picture │      │    Out Loud     │      │   Or Pass Turn  │
└─────────────────┘      └─────────────────┘      └─────────────────┘      └─────────────────┘
```

### Phase 1: Submitting Characters 📝
- Every player privately types a character's name into their phone (e.g., *"Spider-Man"*, *"Beyoncé"*, *"Goku"*, *"SpongeBob"*).
- The app automatically searches Wikipedia and fetches a picture for your character!
- Tap **Submit**.
- When everyone has submitted, the app shuffles and assigns each character to a different player (**nobody gets their own submission**).

---

### Phase 2: Playing Turns & Cards 🃏
- The **Game Board** displays cards for every player in the room:
  - You will see the picture and name of **everyone else's** assigned character.
  - Your own card displays a mystery **`?`** indicator!
- The app randomly picks the first player's turn.

#### On Your Turn:
1. **Ask a Question Out Loud**: Ask the group a **YES/NO** question about your character.
   - *Example*: *"Am I a real person?"*
   - *Example*: *"Am I an animated character?"*
   - *Example*: *"Do I have superpowers?"*
2. **Listen to Responses**: Other players answer *"Yes"* or *"No"* out loud.
3. **Choose Your Action in the App**:
   - 🎯 **Make a Guess**: Type your character's name in the app.
     - **Correct Guess**: Your identity is revealed, you earn a victory checkmark! You solved your character, and the turn automatically moves to the next remaining player.
     - **Wrong Guess**: The app informs you it's incorrect. Your turn ends, and turn passes to the next player.
   - ⏭️ **Pass Turn**: If you aren't sure yet, tap **Pass Turn** to end your turn without guessing.

---

### Phase 3: Private Deduction Notes 🗒️
- Need to keep track of clues? Tap the **Notes** button on your screen to open your private scratchpad!
- Write down clues, ruled-out categories, or ideas (e.g., *"Not human, has cape, male"*).
- **100% Private**: Notes are saved locally on your phone—no other player can read them.

---

### Phase 4: Winning & Game Conclusion 🏆
- Turns continue rotating among active players who haven't solved their character yet.
- Once a player solves their character, they sit back and help answer questions for the remaining players.
- The game ends when **all players have correctly guessed their characters**!

---

## 💡 Gameplay Tips & Rules

- **Forgiving Guessing**: The app accepts minor typos, spacing variations, and common aliases (e.g., *"spiderman"* or *"Spider Man"*, *"Goku"* or *"Son Goku"*).
- **Fair Play**: When answering other players' questions out loud, answer honestly based on what you see on their card!
- **Image Fallback**: If a submitted character is fictional or niche, the app will display a friendly placeholder image if a Wikipedia thumbnail couldn't be found. The character name remains active and playable.

Have fun playing **Who Am I?**! 🎉
